lsi_bot

lsi_bot is the chat bot of LeastSussyImposter's channel, featuring the Wheel of Fortune minigame: spin the wheel, open daily packs, earn coins and spend them on jokers in the shop!

Commands

The command prefix is -.
Commands marked with [@user] default to yourself, or look up the given Twitch user instead.

Wheel of Fortune

Command Description Examples
-wof Spin the Wheel of Fortune!

Most spins are nopes, but hits give you coins, and the rarer the hit, the more coins you get, and maybe a few secrets are hiding in there... 👀
Every spin gives you at least one coin, win or lose.

Long win and loss streaks are rewarded with bonus packs, so keep spinning!

During happy hours, the cooldown of this command is reduced, and more secrets await. See -forecast. 		-wof
-daily Open your daily pack: a bunch of spins at once, plus a pile of coins.
Subscribers and VIPs get more coins from their packs. Following the channel helps too!

A new daily pack is available every day. If you have extra packs, -daily opens them too: they have the same spins, but half the coins of a daily pack. 		-daily

Shop

Spend the coins you earn from -wof and -daily on jokers! Use -shop <joker> with one of the joker names below.

Joker Name Effect Cost Lasts
🎲 Oops! All 6s oops Loaded dice! Fewer nopes, more hits! 200 coins 24 spins
🍀 Celeste's Blessing blessing Celeste is watching over you. Rare hits are twice as likely. 450 coins 15 spins
💀 Plonknondorf's Curse curse Cursed! More nopes, fewer hits. Why would you buy this? 80 coins 24 spins
🪙 Midas Touch midas Everything you touch turns to gold. Your spins give twice the coins. 150 coins 30 spins
  • You can only buy one joker per command.
  • You can't buy a joker you already have active, wait for it to wear off first.
  • Different jokers can be active at the same time, and their effects add up.
  • Jokers wear off after a number of spins. Both -wof spins and each spin of a -daily pack count.
  • Use -balance to see your active jokers and how many spins they have left.
  • Spins with a joker count towards streak bonus packs like any other spin. Pack spins never count towards streaks.
